# Archive credits

Every picture on the archive pages is a photograph of a real print, stencil
or poster, published by the institution that holds it with no rights
reserved (CC0 or public domain), and bundled with the app so nothing is
fetched at run time. Every line of text is quoted from a public-domain
classic. This file lists where each one comes from. Files live in
[`Archive/`](../Archive); the tables that say which one prints on which day
are in `Almanack/Model/ArchivePlates.swift` and `DailyLines.swift`.

roundels, early 19th century, The Metropolitan Museum of Art, Fletcher Fund,

in the page's October persimmon by [`scripts/icon/katagami-icon.swift`](../scripts/icon/katagami-icon.swift).
